Kai's Poem Practice

One poem a day. Read it, then answer the questions — the same steps as your theme paragraph.

How to do it

  1. Read the Glossary box first — just like on the test.
  2. Read the poem out loud once. Tap 🔊 to hear it if you want. A word you don't know? Guess from the words around it and keep going.
  3. Answer the questions. Go back to the poem to check — don't guess.
  4. At the end, tap the words you didn't know. They go into My Words for practice.

Record

    First try → latest try. Doing a whole poem again updates “latest”; “Redo the ones I missed” doesn't count as a try.

      Tap them in the poem. Tap again to take one out.
