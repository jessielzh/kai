📘 Bone groups

Skull 头骨 = cranium (brain case) + mandible (jawbone).

Spinal column 脊柱, from top to bottom:

  1. cervical vertebrae — neck — 7 (C1–C7)
  2. thoracic vertebrae — chest / upper back — 12 (T1–T12)
  3. lumbar vertebrae — lower back — 5 (L1–L5)
  4. sacrum, then coccyx (tailbone)

Hand: carpals (wrist) → metacarpals (palm) → phalanges (fingers).
Foot: tarsals (ankle) → metatarsals (foot) → phalanges (toes).
meta- = “after”: the metacarpals come after the carpals.

Two-bone pairs: forearm = ulna + radius; lower leg = tibia + fibula.
