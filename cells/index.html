📘 Cell facts

What is a cell? The cell 细胞 is the smallest living part of a living thing. All plants and animals are made of cells. Some living things, like bacteria 细菌, have only one cell. People have trillions 万亿 of cells!

A group of cells working together is a tissue 组织. Muscle tissue helps us move 💪. Nerve tissue helps us send messages 📨.

The 3 rules of Cell Theory 细胞学说

  1. All living things are made of one or more cells.
  2. The cell is the basic unit of life. 生命的基本单位
  3. New cells come from cells that already exist.
