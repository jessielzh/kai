How a PEEL paragraph works

P
Point — answer the question and say why.
"The Grand Canal was very significant because it joined north and south China."
E
Evidence — 2 facts: names, dates, numbers.
"It was built in the Sui Dynasty and is over 1,700 km long."
E
Explain — So what? What did it mean for China? Don't repeat the fact!
"This meant that… so China…"
L
Link — go back to the question.
"Therefore, the Grand Canal helped China because…"
